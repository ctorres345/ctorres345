### <img src="yuyay-logo.svg" width="24" height="24" alt="" align="top"> Main project: Yuyay

**Pruebas psicológicas abiertas para Latinoamérica.** An open library of psychological tests for psychology students and professionals in Latin America, starting in Peru. It also shows how many widely used tests still have no adaptation for our countries.

- 📚 [**Yuyay Biblioteca**](https://yuyay.cjtech.dev): see each test's source, license and the countries where it was validated, and try free tests in an educational simulator. No account, no data saved.
- 🩺 [**Yuyay Aplica**](https://yuyay-aplica.cjtech.dev): a panel for professionals to apply tests, in person or on the person's own phone, and see reports and history. Data stays encrypted on the professional's device.
- 🔒 Privacy first: no accounts, no tracking cookies. Works in the browser and installs as an app (PWA). Spanish, English and published Quechua versions.

Built in TypeScript, joining my two paths: software engineering and studying psychology. Contact: [yuyay@cjtech.dev](mailto:yuyay@cjtech.dev)
